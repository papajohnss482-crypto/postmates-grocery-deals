Uber One for Grocery Orders: The Fee Math

Benefits checked in the Postmates app on October 2, 2026: $0 delivery fee on food, grocery and more over the minimum subtotal, up to 10 percent off eligible orders, 5 percent back on rides. Price: 4 week free trial, then $9.99 per month. Students are listed at $4.99 per month with verification, per the program information published in October 2026.

Why grocery is where the membership is most predictable

Restaurant saving mixes membership with set programs and codes, so the value jumps around. Grocery is simpler: most stores do not run a standing discount program, so the delivery fee is the cost that repeats every single order, and the membership removes it on eligible orders over the minimum subtotal. If you order groceries weekly, that one line item is the whole calculation.

Worked example, labeled as an example

Sample numbers, your fees and minimums will differ. Four grocery orders a month, delivery fee $4.99 each, is $19.96 in delivery fees. Against a $9.99 membership, the fee saving alone is about $9.97 ahead per month, before any percentage discount on eligible items. Two orders a month at the same fee is $9.98, a cent under the membership price, so the case then rests on the discounts, not the fees.

What Uber One does not do for groceries

  • It does not remove item markups. A store that prices delivery items above shelf price still does, membership or not. Stores showing in-store prices, like CVS in our Dallas observation of September 26, 2026, are the exception worth preferring.
  • It does not make excluded items eligible. Alcohol, tobacco, and pharmacy exclusions in a promotion's terms still apply.
  • It does not guarantee the minimum subtotal is met. The $0 delivery benefit applies over the program's minimum subtotal, small top-up orders can fall under it.
  • It does not stack with every code. The app decides stacking per offer, and grocery codes plus membership discounts do not always combine.

Who should take it, in grocery terms

  • Weekly grocery order, mid size or bigger: the strongest case. Fees alone usually clear the price.
  • Two orders a month: borderline. Run your own last two receipts through the fee test above before deciding.
  • Mostly small convenience top-ups: weakest case. Minimum subtotals and small baskets eat the benefit.
  • Student ordering regularly: the listed student price of $4.99 per month halves the break-even point. Verify eligibility in the app, student pricing is verified through a student status check.
