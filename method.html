Method and Sources

Field observations, not scraped listings

Every store entry in this tracker was seen on a screen inside the Postmates app by the research account behind this publication, in the city named, on the date named. The Dallas convenience store observations are from September 26, 2026, and they keep that date permanently. Nothing here is copied from a listing site, and nothing is refreshed by editing a date.

Program evidence

The Uber One grocery benefits described on this site, $0 delivery fee on food, grocery and more over the minimum subtotal, up to 10 percent off eligible orders, come from the program screens in the app checked on October 2, 2026, with the standing programs re-checked on October 12, 2026. Where a figure is an example rather than an observation, the page labels it as a worked example.

Why the log is short

A short, dated log is the honest shape of grocery deal data. Store tags rotate weekly and differ by city, so a long list would mostly be a list of things that used to be true somewhere. This tracker would rather publish three verified observations with dates than three hundred undated ones. Readers in other cities should read the log as a method, check your own stores the same way, on the record, with dates.

The source publication

This tracker is the grocery research companion to the main tracking publication, Postmates Coupons, which maintains the full Postmates code list, the in-app program records, and the grocery FAQ this tracker's exclusion rules are drawn from. Where this tracker and the live record differ, the live record is newer.

Corrections

If your app shows a grocery program term that contradicts a page here, trust your app and note the date. Program terms change without announcement, and dated research is only useful if its readers can flag where reality has moved on.

Postmates Grocery Deals Tracker is an independent tracker, not affiliated with Postmates or Uber.
