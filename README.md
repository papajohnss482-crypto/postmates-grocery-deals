# Postmates Grocery Deals Tracker

A dated tracker of grocery and convenience deals on Postmates: store offer tags observed in the app, Uber One grocery value, and the rules that decide whether a grocery promotion applies.

## Purpose (unique to this site)
Grocery. The other stack sites cover the code dataset, savings tools, and existing user research. This one covers only grocery and convenience ordering on Postmates, a topic none of them touch.

## Contents
- index.html: the tracker, dated store observations log, how grocery deals work, current observation table
- uber-one-grocery.html: what Uber One does for grocery orders specifically
- find-grocery-deals.html: step by step guide to finding grocery offers in the app, plus exclusion rules
- method.html: sources and method, observation dates policy

## Data rules
- Observations carry the date and city where they were actually seen. The Dallas store observations are dated September 26, 2026 and will never be relabeled.
- Store offers rotate and are location specific. An observation is a record of what was seen, never a promise of what is live today.
- Full code list and guide: https://postmatescoupons.us/ (source publication, linked as a resource in Method)

## Platform target
Vercel (deployed from this repo). Design: fresh market green theme, no shared template with the other stack sites.
